# List-O-Matic Cloud Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-09

This policy applies to the managed hosted service ("List-O-Matic Cloud").  
Self-hosted use of the open source code is controlled by your own deployment choices.

## What we collect

- Account and billing identifiers needed to run subscriptions.
- Basic service telemetry (for example request health and error diagnostics).
- Company-name query payloads required to run AI matching.

## What we do not intentionally collect in the hosted workflow

- Raw contact PII from your upload workflow is designed to remain in browser-side processing.
- We do not intentionally require full contact rows to execute matching.

## How we use data

- To provide and secure the service.
- To operate billing, account lifecycle, and customer support.
- To monitor reliability and prevent abuse.

## Retention

- We keep account/billing records as needed for operations and legal obligations.
- Operational logs are retained for a limited period based on security and debugging needs.

## Third parties

- We may use infrastructure and payment providers required to operate the managed service.
- Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

## Your choices

- You may cancel your subscription at any time.
- You may request account/data deletion subject to legal and billing retention obligations.

## Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the project maintainers through the support channel published in the product/repository.
